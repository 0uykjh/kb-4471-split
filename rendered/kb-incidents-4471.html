  • parser: the parser reads dates month first, and our tenants write the day first. This stays in #4471. The fix is queued.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints a month-first date hint. Its reports moved to #2.
  • clock: the booking keeps its date and moves one day. Its reports moved to #3.
Split. #4471 is the parser bug only; the other two problems have their own tickets.
